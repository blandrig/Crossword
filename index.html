How to play

1. One click in any square will pop up the associated clue.

2. An option to choose Across or Down will appear when both options are available.

3. When a word is complete and correct it will turn yellow.

4. There are some clues that contain links to the respective song on Spotify.

5. You can check your progress at any time by clicking the Check Puzzle button.

6. You can reveal a single letter by clicking the Reveal button.

7. You can clear the whole puzzle with the Clear All button.
